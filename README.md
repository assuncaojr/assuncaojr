# Assunção Jr

Tech Lead and full-stack engineer with 20+ years building software, from public-sector systems to digital health. Node.js and React on the web, Flutter on mobile. Working remotely from Caxias, Brazil.

## What I've been doing

- **Tech Lead at a Brazilian healthtech** (2023–present). Joined as a senior developer and was promoted to Tech Lead in 2025. I set the technical direction and lead 4 developers and 1 QA. One of our deliveries is patient check-in before each appointment: without it, the payout to the provider is not released, which saves around 30K per day on average.
- **Tech Lead and full-stack engineer at RankMyApp** (2020–2023). Led the squad (1:1s, pair programming, alignment with business goals) and designed Node.js microservices with AWS SQS and RabbitMQ, crawling and scraping, NoSQL databases and caching. With the Product Manager, defined data intelligence features that analyze app performance and visibility.
- **Caxias City Hall** (2002–2023). Internal systems and several versions of the official website, caxias.ma.gov.br. Built an app where citizens send a photo of their medical request, an AI does the first analysis, and a person reviews it to schedule the appointment.

## Built on my own

- **Lembre-me!** Voice reminders: you speak, the phone transcribes it on-device, and if it hears something like "tomorrow at 3pm" the alarm is already set. No server, so it works offline and recordings never leave the device. Flutter, on [Google Play and the App Store](https://revomidia.com.br/apps/lembre-me).
- **[nest-simple-boilerplate](https://github.com/assuncaojr/nest-simple-boilerplate)**. A NestJS starting point with Swagger, env validation, TypeORM and Docker.
